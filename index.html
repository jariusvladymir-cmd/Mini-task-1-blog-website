Artificial Intelligence: The Technology Defining Our Future

By Jarius Vladmir M. De Leon • 4 min read

What Is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence, or AI, is a technology that makes it possible for computers and other machines to carry out the tasks that usually require human intelligence. These tasks include learning, image and speech recognition, language comprehension, problem-solving, decision-making, etc.

Nowadays, AI is not anymore what one might only see in the science fiction films. This technology is already a part of our everyday life.

AI in Everyday Life

There are many cases when people use AI without even knowing it. Asking a virtual assistant questions, receiving recommendations in YouTube or Netflix, finding locations with Google Maps, using facial recognition to unlock a phone are examples of AI at work.

AI can become a very helpful tool in education as well. Students can use AI technologies to explain hard lessons, practice, generate ideas, improve writing skills, etc. However, AI should be considered as a helper in learning process, not as an alternative to human reasoning.

Use of AI to Benefit Our Society

There are many ways how AI can help our society. In healthcare sector, it can be used to analyze images from different devices and recognize diseases. Scientists can use AI to analyze large volumes of information. Businesses can use AI for automating processes and decision-making.

Also, AI can be very useful for disaster recovery. This technology can be used to analyze weather information, identify risks and support emergency management.

Challenges Related to AI

Even though AI gives many advantages, there are also some problems related to it. Sometimes, AI gives wrong information, as well as there are also problems of privacy, misinformation, bias, unemployment, etc.

This is why people need to learn how to use AI responsibly. One should not take everything for granted when it comes to AI technology. One needs to be careful with the information, think critically and use trusted sources.

AI and Our Future

Our future will depend not only on the development of the technology itself but also on its responsible usage by humans. AI can help humans solve problems and reveal possibilities but human intelligence, judgement, empathy and responsibility are still necessary.

For students, learning about AI now can give them an opportunity to be ready for the future where technology will play an even greater role in our lives.

Conclusion

Artificial Intelligence is not just the technology of today but a tool which can revolutionize the way we learn, work, communicate, solve problems, etc. However, the main question about AI is not "What can AI do?" but "How should we use AI responsibly?"

Combining the abilities of AI and human knowledge, creativity, and responsibility can help to use this technology for a better future. AI can shape our future but humans decide what kind of future it will be.
